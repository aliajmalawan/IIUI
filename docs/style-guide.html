The green-on-green problem. Brand primary and semantic success are both green. They are separated three ways: success is brighter and teal-shifted (#0E9F6E vs #14563D); success only ever appears as a soft tinted chip, never as a solid button — solid green means “primary action”; and every semantic state carries an icon plus a text label, so meaning never rests on hue. A success badge and a primary button never sit adjacent.

Body copy is capped at a 68-character measure. A paragraph that runs the full width of a 1440px screen is a readability failure regardless of how much space is available — the eye loses its place on the return sweep. This is why the reading column is narrower than the container that holds it.

--fs-admin-body14px400 / 1.6 · admin base

Admin text sits at 14px with a 1.200 scale. The console is denser because it is operated, not read — a fee clerk scanning 200 challans needs rows, not prose.

Numerals are always tabular. Every table cell, fee amount, mark, date, and KPI uses font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums. Proportional digits make a column of amounts visibly ragged: 12,500 · 8,750 · 141,000 aligns; the same figures in proportional digits do not.

Urdu. [lang="ur"] switches to Noto Nastaliq Urdu at line-height: 2.05 and direction: rtl. Nastaliq descends far below the baseline and clips at normal leading — this is a functional requirement, not a refinement.

Spacing 4px base, 8px rhythm. There are no arbitrary values — margin-top: 18px is a review rejection. If the scale doesn’t fit, the layout is wrong.

Proximity law. The gap above a heading is always at least twice the gap below it. That single ratio is what makes a page scan as grouped blocks instead of an undifferentiated column.

Radius Nesting rule: an inner element’s radius equals the outer radius minus the padding. Concentric corners that ignore padding look wrong even when nobody can say why.

Elevation Every shadow is two-layer — a tight contact shadow plus a wide ambient one, the way real light behaves — and tinted with neutral-950, never pure black.

--shadow-xsInputs, table header
--shadow-smResting cards, navbar
--shadow-mdCard hover, dropdowns
--shadow-lgModals, mega menu
--shadow-xlHero cards, ⌘K palette
--shadow-2xlFull-screen overlays
Border or shadow — never both at the same strength, or the element gets a visible double edge. Hover raises exactly one step and lifts 2px. In dark mode shadows halve and elevation is carried by surface lightness instead: a shadow on a near-black ground is invisible, but a lighter surface reads as closer.

Glass A spotlight technique, not a theme. Permitted on exactly six surfaces; forbidden on tables, forms, the admin sidebar, and any body text.

Sticky navbar over hero · --glass-brand

Deep green tint with a gold hairline border. Always paired with a 1px light edge — without it the panel has no defined boundary and reads as a rendering artefact.

Floating bulk-action bar · --glass-light

Appears above the admin table when rows are selected. Contrast is measured against the lightest pixel behind the glass, not against the tint colour.

Maximum three glass surfaces per viewport — each one forces a separate GPU compositing layer, and backdrop-filter is the single biggest source of jank on low-end Android. An opaque @supports fallback ships with every glass rule, and forced-colors mode drops the blur entirely.

Buttons One primary action per view. One gold element per page, and never inside the admin console — there, primary green is the top of the hierarchy.

Validation timing. Validate on blur first. Once a field has errored, re-validate on input so the user watches the error clear as they fix it. Never validate while someone is still typing their first attempt — it is the fastest way to make a form feel hostile.

Inputs are 16px on mobile. Below 16px, iOS Safari zooms the viewport on focus — a layout bug disguised as a type choice.

Tables The admin console is mostly tables. No zebra striping — with clean dividers it is redundant, and it fights the row hover and selection states.

Numeric columns are right-aligned and tabular-nums, so 141,000 and 12,500 line up on their digits. The selected row carries a tint and a 3px left bar — selection is never signalled by colour alone. Below md, this table becomes stacked cards; columns are never silently hidden, because a value that vanishes is worse than one that needs a scroll.

Alerts & feedback Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it. No apologies, no vagueness.

✓
Challans generated

486 challans created for August 2026. Two students were skipped because they have no active enrolment.

⚠
You are viewing the 2025–2026 session

Marks entered here will not appear in the current session’s results. Switch sessions from the topbar.

✕
Marks exceed the paper total

Row 14 has 87 marks against a 75-mark paper. Correct the value, then save again.

ℹ
Result publication is locked

Chemistry marks for 9-B are still unverified. Results publish once every subject is verified.

Destructive confirmations name the object. “Delete student Ahmad Ali (Reg 2026-0451)?” with an explicit consequence line and a button labelled “Delete Student” — never “Yes”. Irreversible actions such as restoring a backup or cancelling a paid challan additionally require typing a confirmation word.

Navigation The admin sidebar keeps its deep-green ground in both themes — it is a brand anchor, not a content surface.

Academics 🎓 Students 12 📅 Attendance 📓 Examinations Finance 🧾 Fee Challans 86 💰 Payments

The gold bar is the only gold in the console. It marks the active item and ties the admin panel back to the brand without ever competing with a primary action button.

Active state = tinted fill plus a 3px gold left bar plus white semibold text plus aria-current="page". Four signals, because one is never enough.

Collapsed, the rail narrows to 76px with icon-only items and hover tooltips, and the choice is remembered per user. Below lg it becomes an off-canvas overlay.

Session switcher. The admin topbar carries an academic-session pill that turns amber when a non-current session is selected — the single control that prevents the classic “entered this year’s marks into last year” disaster.

Motion Every animation answers “where did this come from?” or “what changed?” Nothing animates to be impressive.

TokenDurationEasingApplied to
--dur-instant0ms—Validation messages, tab switches — feedback must not wait
--dur-fast150msstandardHover, focus, colour change
--dur-base200msease-outDropdowns, tooltips, toasts, modals
--dur-slow300msease-outDrawers, off-canvas, accordions
--dur-slower500msease-outHero entrance, page transitions
--dur-slowest800msease-outKPI count-up, chart first draw
Things enter slower than they leave. A modal opens in 200ms and closes in 150ms; that feels responsive, and the reverse feels sluggish. Only transform and opacity are animated — they are the only GPU-composited properties.

AOS scroll animation is public-site only. An accountant who reloads a table forty times a day does not want it to fade in forty times. Stagger caps at three items × 100ms, and nothing above the fold animates on scroll — that delays perceived load and damages LCP.

prefers-reduced-motion is honoured globally and is a vestibular-disorder accessibility requirement, not a preference: all durations collapse to 1ms, AOS never initialises, the KPI count-up jumps to its final value, and Swiper autoplay is off.
